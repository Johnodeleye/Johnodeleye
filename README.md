**HubPost Community** is the growing ecosystem I'm building to empower developers, designers, creators, and entrepreneurs across Africa to connect, grow, and showcase their talents to the world.

### 🔧 Key Features:
- **Visibility-driven social system** - Where follower count doesn't define reach
- **Built-in marketplace** with escrow protection for scam-free trading
- **Creator tools** for portfolios, CTAs, and business offers
- **Community spaces** for collaboration across tech, music, design, and business niches
- **Zero-scam algorithm** ensuring safe digital transactions

### 🌎 Our Mission:
To make visibility and digital trust accessible to everyone - from Africa to the world - by building a platform where creators thrive, communities grow, and connections are real.
